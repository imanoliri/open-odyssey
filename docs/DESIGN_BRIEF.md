# Open Odyssey design brief

## Promise

Every flight is a physical expedition through a dangerous landscape, not merely
travel between markers.

## Experience pillars

1. **The aircraft is a character.** It has readable weight, momentum, limits,
   strengths, and vulnerabilities.
2. **Terrain creates gameplay.** Valleys, ridges, narrow passes, weather, and
   improvised runways produce meaningful piloting decisions.
3. **Adventure matters more than combat.** Navigation, delivery, rescue,
   discovery, endurance, and precision are primary verbs.
4. **Risk is legible.** Sound, camera, vibration, instruments, and animation
   warn the player before failure.
5. **Mastery remains approachable.** The model is coherent and learnable without
   requiring professional flight-simulator knowledge.

## First vertical slice

Depart a coastal runway, carry expedition supplies through a turbulent mountain
pass, and land at a remote camp. Target duration is five minutes.

## Deliberate differences

- Original world, aircraft, missions, names, art direction, story, and music.
- Modern accessibility and remappable controls.
- Data-driven aircraft and mission definitions.
- Desktop and browser builds from one project.
- Telemetry and repeatable tests for deliberate tuning.

## Non-goals for the first slice

- Complete decompilation of a PS2 executable
- Extraction or reuse of original assets
- Real-world certified aerodynamics
- Open-world streaming
- Multiple aircraft
- Combat
- Final visual art
